confessionsat1am uploader

A personal, single-user desktop tool. It uploads the operator's own original fictional story videos to the operator's own TikTok account (@confessionsat1am) as drafts. The operator reviews each draft in the TikTok app and publishes it manually. The tool has no other users and does not access anyone else's data.
